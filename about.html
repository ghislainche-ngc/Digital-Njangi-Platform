About NjangiBridge

NjangiBridge helps Njangi groups manage collections, payouts, and transparent accounting. We integrate with mobile money providers and provide notification tooling to keep your group informed.

Our Mission

To make informal savings groups more reliable and transparent using simple digital tools tailored to African mobile money ecosystems.
